Privacy policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Northstar helps people find shelter, food and work near them, and helps volunteers give help. This page explains what information Northstar collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

You can use Northstar without an account

As a guest you can see the map, call for help and read job listings. Nothing you do as a guest is saved on our servers.

What we collect

  • Your account. The name you choose, your password (stored only as a scrambled code we can’t read), and whether you use Northstar to find help or give help. We don’t ask for your email, ID or address.
  • What you save in the app. Places you save, the answers you give the resume builder and the resume it makes, your progress, donations you record, and your app settings. These are saved with your account so they’re there on any phone.
  • What you post. Jobs you post (title, pay, place, how to contact you) and food pickup requests (what you’re giving, the area, and any note you add). Other people can see your posts. Only you and the volunteer who claims a pickup can see your pickup note.
  • Your location, only when you use it. If you let Northstar use your location, it’s used on your phone to sort places by distance. When you ask for bus or walking directions, your location and the place are sent to our server and to Google Maps to plan the route. Location is never stored with your account.
  • Companion chats and voice, only if you allow it. See the next section.
  • Reports. If you report a post, we keep the report and your account name so our team can review it.

Companion, the AI helper

Companion only works after you tap Allow. Before that, nothing you type or say is sent anywhere.

  • What you type to Companion, and your recent messages in that chat, are sent to Google’s Gemini AI service to write an answer.
  • If you use your voice, your recording is sent to ElevenLabs to turn it into words, and Companion’s answers are sent to ElevenLabs to be read out loud.
  • Please don’t share things you want to keep private, like ID numbers or health details.
  • You can turn Companion off at any time in Settings → Companion AI. Nothing more is sent after that.

The resume builder sends your answers to Northstar’s own resume service to write your resume.

Who we share information with

Only with the services that run Northstar, and only what each needs:

  • Supabase stores accounts, saved app data and posts.
  • Google (Gemini for Companion, if you allow it; Google Maps for directions you ask for).
  • ElevenLabs for Companion’s voice, if you allow it.
  • Stripe, if you donate money. Payment happens on Stripe’s secure page, outside the app. We never see your card number.
  • Employers’ websites, only when you choose to contact them or tap Fill in for me, which puts your name, phone and email into their form.

Job listings from other websites are read by our server; none of your information is sent to those websites unless you contact the employer yourself. Every service we share information with must protect it at least as well as this policy does. We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account and saved data until you delete your account. To delete it, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your saved places, resume and progress, and the jobs and pickup requests you posted. A pickup a volunteer is already delivering stays visible to them, but is no longer linked to you. Reports are kept so we can keep the app safe.

Children

Northstar isn’t made for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change affects you.

Contact us

Questions about your privacy, or a post that worries you? Use Report on the post, or contact us at the address shown in Settings.
